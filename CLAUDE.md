## What this is

AI-powered personal health platform (demo mode, no backend): condition-aware meal plans, workouts, recipes, lifestyle tips, progress tracking, email/PDF report. Next.js 15.5 static export, all logic client-side.

- `frontend/` — the whole app. Build: `cd frontend && npm run build` (run `npm install` first if node_modules is missing — container resets wipe it).
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_MODE=true` in `.env.production` → everything generated client-side by the recommendation engine.
- Email report needs EmailJS env vars in Vercel (`NEXT_PUBLIC_EMAILJS_SERVICE_ID/TEMPLATE_ID/PUBLIC_KEY`) — placeholders in `.env.production`; PDF download works without setup (html2canvas + jspdf, dynamic imports).

## Data-shape gotchas (caused real bugs before)

- Meal plan has `plan.meals[]` (NOT `plan.slots[]`); items have `item.food.id` (prefixed `food-`) and `item.food.name` (NOT `item.food_id` / `item.name`).
- Recipe lookup: strip prefix → `foodId.replace(/^food-/, "")`.
- Diet values: `vegetarian | non_vegetarian | vegan | pescatarian`; Indian vegetarian = NO eggs (handled by `eggAllowed()`).
- Goals include `diabetes_friendly` and `blood_pressure_management` — any new `Record<goal, …>` map must cover all 8 goals in `constants.ts`.
- CKD is a HARD clinical cap: total plan protein must never exceed 0.75 g/kg after any scaling change. The sweep test checks this.
- **`isCarbControlled(input)` is the ONE definition** of a carb-ceiling plan (diabetes_friendly goal, T2D, PREDIABETES, HYPERTRIGLYCERIDEMIA), used by computeMacros, the plan builder and the Copilot. They used to decide separately, which is how a 40% ceiling got computed and quoted but never enforced.
- Food `satfat` labels must agree with the grams in `nutrition-data` (>= 6 g/serving is "high") — the cholesterol exclusion keys off the label. A test enforces it.
- **Two hosting targets:** Vercel (production, from `main`) and GitHub Pages (`deploy-pages.yml` -> `gh-pages`, built with `GITHUB_PAGES=true` for a `/Amit33-design` basePath). Vercel must never build `gh-pages` — see round 21. **Owner decision (2026-09-26): keep the GitHub Pages copy** — it is an intentional copy for initial testing; do not propose removing it. ## How the meal engine works (current design)

1. **computeMacros**: BMR → TDEE → goal calorie adj (+ safe floor 1200♀/1500♂) → goal protein g/kg (CKD override 0.75) → goal fat% → carbs remainder (≤40% cal for diabetes).
2. **Phase 1 select**: per slot, filter safe foods (diet, cuisine w/ fallback, egg rule, condition excludes, GI>=70 for carb-controlled plans), rank by preferenceScore + seeded daily jitter, pick anchor-first. Then the **omega-3 guarantee** (add the cheapest-calorie ALA/fish source if the day has none).
   **Phase 1b swaps** (in order): sodium; saturated fat (lipid plans); carb share (carb-controlled, may cross food groups); potassium (CKD). Each keeps the plate's shape and guards against creating another deficiency (iron, sat fat, sodium, carbs, protein floor, last omega-3 source).
3. **Phase 2 portion-scale**: decoupled levers — protein-dense items steered to protein target, energy items to remaining calories, within per-group `scaleBounds`; drop items for very low targets; CKD trim/drop; **carb lever** (shrink carb-dense items relative to target share, drop only when carbs AND calories are over, regrow fat/protein under their ceilings, never below the clinical protein floor 1.0 g/kg / 1.1 over 65); **sat-fat shrink** (runs BEFORE the top-up); then the **calorie top-up** (carb-aware, sat-fat-density-aware, never stretches oils on lipid plans). Every drop pass protects a main meal's last vegetable AND the day's last omega-3 source (`carriesLastVegetable`).
4. **Phase 3 materialise**: `toMealItem(food, slot, scale)` → scaled qty/macros + `serving_scale`; plan returns `fit` {calories, protein, carbs, fat, overall %} shown as "Plan Match" strip on nutrition page.

- **2026-09-24 (19)** Backlog #1 (CKD potassium) and #4 (biceps). Two fixes, one honest non-fix.
  **Potassium, 197% -> 168% of the renal limit, at zero cost to anything else.** Two mechanisms, both mirroring machinery that already works elsewhere in the engine:
  (1) **Leaching is now counted, because the plan already prescribes it.** The engine has long modelled reduced-salt cooking (nova>=3 dishes get 62.5% of listed sodium for HTN/CKD). Boiling vegetables and pulses in a large volume of water and discarding it removes a comparable share of potassium — it is water-soluble and this is the standard renal kitchen technique — but nothing counted it, even though the app's own potassium guidance told users to do it. `sumMicros` takes `leachedVegetables` (CKD only, `vegetable`/`legumes` groups only, **30%** — deliberately the conservative end of the published 30-50% range, because overstating it hands a patient a number their blood test will not agree with). Crucially the copy now says so out loud on both branches: "that figure ALREADY assumes you boil vegetables and pulses in plenty of water and pour the water away — if you cook them any other way the real number is higher." Banking a reduction the user does not know about would be dishonest.
  (2) **A potassium swap pass for renal plans**, the same Phase 1b move that worked for sodium: replace the highest-potassium dish with a lower-potassium option the slot already offers, same group, same anchor role, vegetables preserved, and never raising sodium by more than 100 mg so it cannot undo the sodium work.
  **A self-inflicted regression the measurement caught:** the first version of the swap cost CKD plans 3 points of calorie delivery (87% -> 84%) because repeatedly choosing lower-potassium dishes tends to choose smaller ones — the exact harm round 16 was about, since under-feeding on a protein-restricted diet burns lean tissue and raises urea. Added `to.cal >= from.cal * 0.9`. Potassium gain drops 40 -> 29 points but delivery returns to 87% and the worst case actually improves (49% -> 54%). The smaller win without the harm is the better trade.
  **Biceps closed** (backlog #4): a band curl in `wo-senior-standing` takes older adults from 4.5 sets (under a 6-16 target) to 7.5-12. No muscle is now under range for any senior profile.
  Verified against a stashed HEAD rather than assumed: non-CKD identical on every metric, CKD calorie fit 86% -> 87%, ALL delivery min 49% -> 54%, sodium unchanged, overall fit unchanged at 92%. 107 tests.
  **The honest non-fix — and a decision for the owner, not for me.** Even after both fixes, **0 of 60 CKD plans land under 2000 mg**; the mean is 2798 mg on the profile I rendered. The remaining potassium is moderate-potassium whole foods, and squeezing it further means either gutting the vegetables and pulses CKD patients also benefit from, or adding lower-potassium dish variants to the library. - **2026-09-13 (16)** Swept weekly nutrient consistency across the whole profile space for the first time and it exposed the most serious defect found so far: **CKD plans delivered 49% of their calorie target** (worst 35% — a 2460 kcal prescription served as 869 kcal).
  Why it hid: `invariants.test.ts` checked that the macro TARGET clears BMR and that plan protein stays under the renal cap. Clinically this is backwards in the direction that matters: under-eating on a protein-restricted diet makes the body catabolise lean tissue, which RAISES urea — exactly what the protein cap exists to prevent.
  Mechanism: the CKD trim shrinks protein-bearing items to their lower bound and drops protein-dense dishes to respect the cap, removing all their energy with them. The non-CKD overshoot path ends with `steer(energyItems, ...)` to refill; **the renal path had no refill at all.**
  Fix, in layers: (1) a calorie top-up pass that re-stretches near-zero-protein items and then ADDS low-protein energy from each slot's full safe pool (not just its ten alternatives); (2) the library had no `fats` group whatsoever and every grain in it is whole-grain (5-18 g protein), so a renal plan had nothing to make up calories with — added `olive-oil`, `ghee` (satfat high, so heart/lipid profiles exclude it) and `white-rice` (GI 73, so T2D excludes it) with nutrient rows and a recipe; (3) generalised the top-up to ALL plans, which also lifted non-CKD worst-case delivery 78% → 87%.
  Measured against a stashed baseline rather than assumed: CKD overall fit 73% → 89%, CKD calorie delivery 57% → 87%, non-CKD overall fit 93% → 93% (unchanged), non-CKD delivery min 78% → 87%. New invariant: every plan delivers >=85% of target **or** carries an explicit shortfall warning.
  **Four self-inflicted bugs on the way, all found by measuring rather than reasoning** — worth remembering because each looked correct in the diff: (a) the pass added straight to `picked`, bypassing `tryAdd` and therefore the weekly variety cap — olive oil served 21x/week; cooking fats are now deliberately EXEMPT from that cap (a drizzle is not a dish) and everything else honours it. (b) `steer()` moves a total TOWARD a target, so calling it after an add could SHRINK the item just added — 2002 kcal collapsed back to 1699; added a grow-only `steerUp`. (c) the renal cap-correction was not gated on `proteinCap`, so on ordinary plans (where protein legitimately sits above target, ceiling is 1.6x) it crushed the stretched energy items — this is what made three profiles keep failing after the "fix".
